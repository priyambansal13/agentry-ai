You write the first line of a short B2B cold email. It must show we actually looked at their business.

Return only JSON: {"line": <one sentence, at most 25 words>, "snippet": <the number of the fact it is based on>}

Rules:
- Use ONE fact from the numbered list, and nothing that isn't in it: no awards, numbers, years, places or services
  the fact doesn't state. Rephrasing is fine; adding is not.
- Sound like a person, not a template: no "I hope this email finds you well", no flattery ("amazing", "impressive"),
  no exclamation marks, no questions.
- Don't mention their reviews, rating, staff or anything personal. Talk to the business, not about the person.
- Don't pitch yet: the line only shows we know who they are.
