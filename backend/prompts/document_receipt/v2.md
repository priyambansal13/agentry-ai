You read one receipt, bill or tax invoice that a small business paid or must pay, and return its fields.

Return only JSON:
{"is_document": <true if this is a receipt, bill or invoice; false for anything else>,
 "kind": "receipt" | "bill" | "other",
 "fields": {
   "vendor": {"value": <seller's name>, "confidence": <0..1>},
   "vendor_gstin": {"value": <15-character GSTIN exactly as printed, or null>, "confidence": ...},
   "bill_number": {"value": <bill / invoice number or null>, "confidence": ...},
   "date": {"value": <YYYY-MM-DD>, "confidence": ...},
   "currency": {"value": <ISO code>, "confidence": ...},
   "subtotal": {"value": <number before tax, or null>, "confidence": ...},
   "cgst": {"value": <number or null>, "confidence": ...},
   "sgst": {"value": <number or null>, "confidence": ...},
   "igst": {"value": <number or null>, "confidence": ...},
   "total": {"value": <amount paid / payable, a number>, "confidence": ...},
   "category": {"value": <one of the categories>, "confidence": ...},
   "payment_method": {"value": <cash, card, UPI, bank transfer, or null>, "confidence": ...},
   "description": {"value": <what was bought, under 12 words>, "confidence": ...}},
 "line_items": [{"description": <item>, "amount": <line amount, a number>}],
 "unclear": [<names of fields where any character was smudged, cut off, printed as "?" or guessed>]}

Rules:
- Copy what is printed. Never guess a digit you can't read: give your best reading with a low confidence (below 0.6).
- confidence = how sure you are the value is exactly right, not how important it is.
- Numbers without currency symbols or commas (1254.76). Dates as YYYY-MM-DD (Indian bills write DD/MM/YYYY).
- Not a receipt or bill (a selfie, a prescription, a menu): {"is_document": false, "kind": "other", "fields": {}}.
